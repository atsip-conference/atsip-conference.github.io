The 4th edition was held in Sousse, Tunisia, 21–24 March 2018, organised by the
ATMS laboratory. It was paired with a spring school, SS-ATSIP'2018.

## Programme

Eight regular tracks: speech and audio; image and video; remote sensing and
assimilation; modelling in signals and images; medical images and
applications; data fusion; signal processing for communications and
networking; and embedded systems, RF and applications.

Three special sessions, the first two marking the arrival of deep learning in
the programme:

- Deep learning for medical imaging and health informatics — Rachid Jennane
  (Université d'Orléans) and Mohammed El Hassouni (Mohammed V University, Rabat)
- Deep learning for remote sensing images — Abdelmalek Toumi (ENSTA Bretagne)
- Unconventional tools for smart signal and image processing — Nawres Khlifa
  (ISTM) and Hassene Seddik (ENSIT)

## Special issues

Selected papers were invited to the *European Journal of Remote Sensing*,
*Multimedia Tools and Applications*, and the *International Journal of RF and
Microwave Computer-Aided Engineering*.

Proceedings are published by IEEE and indexed in IEEE Xplore. ISBN 978-1-5386-5239-8.
