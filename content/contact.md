For questions about ATSIP'2027 — submissions, special sessions, registration —
write to **atsip.conf@gmail.com**.

ATSIP'2027 is organised by the ATISP laboratory, ENET'Com, University of Sfax,
Tunisia, with the ATSI association and the IEEE Tunisia Section.

The conference was founded in 2014 by Professor Ahmed Ben Hamida and the ATMS
laboratory (ENIS, University of Sfax).
