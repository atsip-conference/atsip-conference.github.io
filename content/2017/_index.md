The 3rd edition was held in Fez, Morocco, 22–24 May 2017 — the first time the
conference was held outside Tunisia. It was dedicated to the memory of
Professor Driss Aboutajdine, honorary chair of the conference.

## Programme

Four regular tracks, widened for this edition to take in embedded systems:
speech, signal and image processing; embedded systems and applications; remote
sensing and assimilation; and medical images and applications. The chairs were
drawn from Moroccan and Tunisian institutions together.

Five special sessions:

- Visual quality assessment: tools and applications
- 3D human tracking and activity recognition
- Multimedia data analysis and security
- Wireless sensor networks: energy consumption management
- Machine learning in medical imaging

Submissions were handled through EasyChair.

## Special issues

Selected papers were invited to the *European Journal of Remote Sensing* and
to *Multimedia Tools and Applications* (Springer).

Proceedings are published by IEEE and indexed in IEEE Xplore. ISBN 978-1-5386-0551-6.
