---
title: "Sponsors and partners"
---

ATSIP'2027 is organised with the support of institutions and companies working
in signal processing, imaging and related technologies.

Sponsorship supports the doctoral programme, keeps registration affordable for
students, and funds the invited speakers. Packages range from exhibition space
to named sessions and awards.

To discuss sponsorship, write to the organising committee — see the
[contact page]({{< relref "/contact" >}}).
