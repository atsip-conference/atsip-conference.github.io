The 2nd edition was held in Monastir, Tunisia, 21–23 March 2016, at the
SENTIDO Rosa Beach hotel, organised by the ATMS laboratory.

## Programme

Eight regular tracks, each led by a pair or trio of topic chairs drawn from
Tunisia, France, Switzerland, Canada and Qatar, covering speech and audio,
image and video, remote sensing, modelling, medical imaging, data fusion,
communications and networking, and cryptography and watermarking.

Seven special sessions were held alongside them:

- Big data processing and the Internet of Things
- Signal and image processing for emotion recognition
- Radio-frequency identification
- Optical remote sensing signal modelling
- Functional medical image processing
- Natural images and document image processing
- Processing of words and text from image and electronic documents

The first of these was paired with a tutorial and round table led by Professor
Yahya Slimani (ISAMM, Tunis).

## Special issues

Selected remote sensing papers were invited to extend their work for the
*European Journal of Remote Sensing*.

Proceedings are published by IEEE and indexed in IEEE Xplore. ISBN 978-1-4673-8526-8.
