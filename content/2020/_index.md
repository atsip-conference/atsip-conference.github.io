The 5th edition was held in Sousse, Tunisia, 2–5 September 2020, organised by
the ATMS laboratory. It was the only edition held in September: the date was
moved from its usual spring slot, and the conference went ahead in the middle
of the pandemic year.

## Programme

Eight regular tracks, with chairs from Tunisia, France, Morocco and Canada:
speech and audio; image and video; remote sensing and assimilation; modelling
in signals and images; medical images and applications; data fusion; signal
processing for communications and networking; and embedded systems, RF and
applications.

Four special sessions:

- Modern standard and dialectal Arabic speech synthesis — Gérard Chollet
  (CNRS-SAMOVAR, Institut Polytechnique de Paris), Zied Lachiri (ENIT) and
  Fathy Yassa (Speech Morphing Inc.)
- Unconventional tools for smart signal and image processing — Nawres Khlifa
  (ISTM) and Hassene Seddik (ENSIT)
- Data and information quality — Sonda Ammar Bouhamed and Imen Khanfir Kallel
  (University of Sfax)
- Medical applications for clinical assistance based on EEG and EMG signals —
  Hassene Seddik (ENSIT), Itaf Ben Slimen and Monaam Ayachi

## Special issues

Selected papers were put forward for the *Journal of Medical Imaging* (SPIE),
*Multimedia Tools and Applications*, and the *European Journal of Remote
Sensing*.

Proceedings are published by IEEE and indexed in IEEE Xplore. ISBN 978-1-7281-7513-3.
