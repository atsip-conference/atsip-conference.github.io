## How to submit

Submissions for ATSIP'2027 are handled electronically through **Microsoft CMT**.

Authors need a CMT account before submitting. Accounts are free and can be
created at [cmt3.research.microsoft.com](https://cmt3.research.microsoft.com).

## Format

- **Six pages maximum**, including results, figures, and references
- IEEE conference format (two columns, A4)
- PDF only
- Original work, not published elsewhere and not under concurrent review

## Review

Each submission receives independent reviews from members of the technical
programme committee.

## After acceptance

Authors of accepted papers must:

1. Upload a camera-ready version addressing the reviewers' comments
2. Complete the IEEE electronic copyright form
3. Register at least one author for the conference

Papers that are not presented at the conference by a registered author are not
submitted to IEEE Xplore.
