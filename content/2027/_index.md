<div class="lead">

The **8th International Conference on Advanced Technologies, Signal and Image
Processing (ATSIP'2027)** will be held in **Sousse, Tunisia, from 30 June to
3 July 2027**.

The conference is organised by the **ATISP laboratory (ENET'Com, University of
Sfax)** with the ATSI association and the IEEE Tunisia Section. Every edition since 2014
has been published by IEEE and indexed in IEEE Xplore.

## Topics

Main topics, with subtopics, include but are not limited to:

- Medical signal/image and computer-aided diagnosis
- IoT, embedded systems and RF applications
- Remote sensing and assimilation
- New technologies for communications and networking
- Image, video, speech and audio processing
- Big data and cloud computing applications
- AI for advanced technologies
- Advanced technologies for security

Machine learning and deep learning contributions are welcome across all tracks.
[Full call for papers, with track chairs]({{< relref "/2027/call-for-papers" >}}).

## Author guidelines

Papers are limited to **six pages** including results, figures and references, in
IEEE conference format. Submissions must report original work that is not
published elsewhere and not under concurrent review.

Each submission receives independent reviews from members of the technical
programme committee. Accepted papers must be presented by a registered author to
appear in the proceedings, and are submitted for inclusion in IEEE Xplore.

**Templates:** [Word and LaTeX](https://www.ieee.org/conferences/publishing/templates.html)
&middot; [Overleaf](https://www.overleaf.com/latex/templates/preparation-of-papers-for-ieee-sponsored-conferences-and-symposia/zfnqfzzzxghk)
&middot; [Template Selector](https://template-selector.ieee.org/) — use the A4 variant.
