The 8th International Conference on Advanced Technologies, Signal and Image
Processing will be held in Sousse, Tunisia, from 30 June to 3 July 2027.

The conference is organised by the ATMS laboratory with the ATSI association and
the IEEE Tunisia Section. Accepted and presented papers will be submitted for
inclusion in IEEE Xplore.
