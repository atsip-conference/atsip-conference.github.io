ATSIP'2027 is held in **Sousse, Tunisia**, from 30 June to 3 July 2027.

<img class="banner" src="{{< relref "/" >}}img/sousse.png" alt="Sousse: medina souk, golf course and marina">

Sousse sits on the Gulf of Hammamet, about 140 km south of Tunis. Its medina is
a UNESCO World Heritage site, and the city is served by Monastir airport (MIR),
20 km away, and by Tunis–Carthage (TUN), two hours by road or rail.
