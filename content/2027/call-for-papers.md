The 8th International Conference on Advanced Technologies for Signal and Image
Processing invites original contributions on signal processing, image analysis,
and their applications.

The topics below extend the list used across previous editions. Learning-based
and AI methods are welcome throughout: they appear as subtopics within each
track rather than as a separate category, since they now cut across the whole
field.

## Topics for this edition

ATSIP&rsquo;2027 builds on the [eight general tracks of the conference]({{< relref "/" >}}#topics)
and gives particular emphasis to the areas below.

### Emphasis areas

- Foundation models and self-supervised learning for signals and images
- Trustworthy AI: calibration, uncertainty, and interpretability
- Medical imaging and computer-aided diagnosis
- Federated, edge, and resource-constrained learning
- Generative models and synthetic data
- Security and adversarial robustness of learned systems

Papers on any of the eight general tracks remain equally welcome; the areas
above indicate where the programme committee expects the strongest activity in
2027, not a restriction on scope.

## Submission

Papers are limited to six pages including figures and references, in IEEE
conference format. Submissions must report original work that is not published
elsewhere and not under concurrent review.

All submissions receive independent reviews from members of the technical
programme committee. Accepted papers must be presented by a registered author to
appear in the proceedings.

Accepted and presented papers will be submitted for inclusion in IEEE Xplore.
