The 1st edition was held in Sousse, Tunisia, 17–19 March 2014, at the Hotel El
Mouradi Palm Marina. It was organised by the ATMS laboratory (Advanced
Technologies for Medicine and Signals), on the initiative of Professor Ahmed
Ben Hamida, who founded the series.

## Plenary lectures

- **Jean-Paul Haton** — Recent advances in automatic speech recognition
- **Gérard Chollet** — Audio-visual speech processing
- **Sylvie Le Hégarat** — Belief functions for image analysis and processing
- **Basel Solaiman** — Possibility theory and scene interpretation

## Programme

Eight tracks: speech and audio processing; image and video processing; remote
sensing and assimilation; modelling in signals and images; medical images and
applications; data fusion; signal processing for communications and
networking; and cryptography, watermarking and steganography. The programme
combined lecture and poster sessions with tutorials and an exhibition.

Papers were limited to six pages, the format the series has kept since.

Proceedings are published by IEEE and indexed in IEEE Xplore.
