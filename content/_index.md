---
title: "ATSIP"
---

ATSIP brings together researchers and engineers working on signal processing,
image analysis, and their applications. The conference has been held since 2014,
with proceedings published by IEEE and indexed in IEEE Xplore.

The 2027 edition returns to Sousse. After a three-year interval the series moves
to a new rhythm; ATSIP'2027 is the eighth edition.
