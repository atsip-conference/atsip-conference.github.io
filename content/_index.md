## About the conference

ATSIP is an international conference on signal processing, image analysis, and
their applications. Held since 2014, it brings together researchers, engineers,
and doctoral students from the Euro-Mediterranean region and beyond.

Every edition since 2014 has been published by IEEE and indexed in IEEE Xplore.
The complete bibliographic record of the series is maintained in DBLP, and the
conference is listed in the CORE conference portal.

The conference was founded in 2014 by Professor Ahmed Ben Hamida, head of the
Advanced Technologies for Medicine and Signals laboratory (ATMS) at the National
Engineering School of Sfax, and has been held under his direction since.

## Aims and scope

<!-- À COMPLÉTER OU CONSERVER : reformuler selon la politique du comité. -->

The conference exists to give the signal and image processing community of the
region a regular, indexed venue of its own — one where doctoral students present
alongside established researchers, and where work rooted in local problems reaches
an international readership.

Three commitments shape it:

- **A genuine review process.** Every submission receives independent reviews from
  members of the technical programme committee.
- **Doctoral training.** Several editions have been paired with a doctoral school,
  giving students a full programme of tutorials alongside the technical sessions.
- **Regional anchoring, international reach.** Held across Tunisia and Morocco,
  with authors and committee members drawn from a wider community.

## Topics

ATSIP covers eight main tracks, carried forward across every edition since 2014
and extended to reflect the place that learning-based methods now hold in the
field. Machine learning and deep learning are welcome throughout, as a subject
of study and as a method applied to established problems.

Each edition may add special sessions on focused topics, announced in the call
for papers for that edition.

## History

The first edition was held in Sousse in March 2014, on the initiative of
Professor Ahmed Ben Hamida and the ATMS laboratory. Six editions followed, hosted
in Monastir, Fez, Sousse, and Sfax — the 2017 edition in Morocco marking the first
time the conference was held outside Tunisia.

Several editions were accompanied by a doctoral school, held immediately before
or after the main programme.

ATSIP'2027 is the eighth edition and returns to Sousse.

## Organisation

The conference was founded and is headed by Professor Ahmed Ben Hamida, director
of the ATMS laboratory (Advanced Technologies for Medicine and Signals, ENIS,
University of Sfax).
