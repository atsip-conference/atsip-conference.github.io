## About the conference

ATSIP is an international conference on signal processing, image analysis, and
their applications. Held since 2014, it brings together researchers, engineers,
and doctoral students from the Euro-Mediterranean region and beyond.

The conference was founded in 2014 by Professor Ahmed Ben Hamida, head of the
ATMS laboratory at the National Engineering School of Sfax, and has been held
under his direction since.

Every edition since 2014 has been published by IEEE and indexed in IEEE Xplore.
The complete bibliographic record of the series is maintained in DBLP, and the
conference is listed in the CORE conference portal.

## Aims and scope

<!-- À VALIDER PAR LE COMITÉ : proposition de rédaction, à confirmer ou réécrire. -->

The conference exists to give the signal and image processing community of the
region a regular, indexed venue of its own — one where doctoral students present
alongside established researchers, and where work rooted in local problems
reaches an international readership.

Three commitments shape it:

- **A genuine review process.** Every submission receives independent reviews
  from members of the technical programme committee.
- **Doctoral training.** Several editions have been paired with a doctoral
  school, giving students a full programme of tutorials alongside the technical
  sessions.
- **Regional anchoring, international reach.** Held across Tunisia and Morocco,
  with authors and committee members drawn from a wider community.

## Topics

ATSIP covers eight main tracks. Each edition may add special sessions on focused
topics, announced in the call for papers for that edition.

- Embedded architectures for signal and image processing

- Satellite image processing
- Multiresolution fusion and pansharpening

- MIMO systems and space-time coding
- Multi-carrier and multi-rate systems
- Signal processing for ad-hoc and sensor networks

## History

The first edition was held in Sousse in March 2014, on the initiative of
Professor Ahmed Ben Hamida and the ATMS laboratory. Six editions followed, hosted
in Monastir, Fez, Sousse, and Sfax — the 2017 edition in Morocco marking the
first time the conference was held outside Tunisia.

Several editions were accompanied by a doctoral school, held immediately before
or after the main programme.

ATSIP'2027 is the eighth edition and returns to Sousse.

## Organisation

The conference was founded and is headed by Professor Ahmed Ben Hamida, director
of the ATMS laboratory (ENIS, University of Sfax), and is organised with the ATSI
association and the IEEE Tunisia Section.
